# Store listing copy

Paste-ready, within Play's limits. Character counts are in the headings.

The strategy, in one line: **every large competitor in this category runs ads,
requires an account, or has been caught selling location data.** Sabeel does
none of those, and that is the whole pitch. Lead with it in the title, prove it
in the first two lines, and let the features follow.

---

## App name (30 characters)

```
Sabeel: Quran, Prayer & Dua
```

27 characters. The name alone means nothing to someone browsing, so the three
words after it are what the search index and the eye both get. Do not pad it
with keywords — Play penalises that, and it reads as spam to a human.

---

## Short description (80 characters)

This is the line under the title in search results, and it is the highest-value
text in the whole listing.

```
Quran, hadith, prayer times & dua. No ads, no account, no tracking. Offline.
```

76 characters, inside Play's 80. Every word is a differentiator rather than a
category label — "Quran app" tells a browser nothing that the title has not
already said.

## What to actually compete on

The apps above Sabeel in this category are beaten on trust, not on features.

- **Muslim Pro** — the largest, and the most disliked. Heavy ads, a subscription,
  and a 2020 scandal over location data reaching US defence contractors. Anyone
  who remembers that is looking for a replacement.
- **Quran Majeed** — good, but paid features and ads.
- **Athan / IslamicFinder** — ad-supported.
- **Tarteel** — excellent at its one thing, subscription-priced.
- **Quran.com** — trusted, but needs a connection for much of it.

So do not try to out-feature them. Own the sentence they cannot say:

> **No ads. No account. No tracking. Works offline. Free forever.**

Repeat it in the title, the short description, the first line, the screenshots
and the data-safety card. A listing that says "no data collected" next to a
competitor's long list of collected data is the most persuasive thing on the
page, and it costs nothing because it is simply true.

## After launch

- **Reply to every review for the first month.** Play weighs this, and it is the
  cheapest credibility available.
- **Ship small updates often.** Recency counts in ranking.
- **Ask for a rating after a good moment** — a completed khatm day, a week's
  streak — never on launch.
- **Localise the listing** into Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Malay and
  Bengali. Translating *the listing* is cheap and opens enormous markets; it can
  be done before the app itself is translated.
