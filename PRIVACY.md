# Privacy Policy — Sabeel

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

Sabeel collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and no server that belongs to this app.

This is not a promise about how carefully data is handled. There is no data to
handle, because none is ever sent anywhere.

---

## What stays on your device

Everything you do in Sabeel is stored on your phone and nowhere else:

| What | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| Your location, for prayer times and the qibla | Your device only |
| Which prayers you have marked as prayed | Your device only |
| Bookmarks, notes and your last-read position | Your device only |
| Your khatm plan and reading streak | Your device only |
| Settings — theme, fonts, reciter, adhan choices | Your device only |
| Recitation audio you have played or downloaded | Your device's app storage |

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, which
also means there is nothing to restore — export a backup from Settings first if
you want to keep it.

## Location

If you allow it, Sabeel reads your location to calculate prayer times and the
direction of the qibla. The calculation happens on your phone, using an
astronomical formula and a magnetic model that ship inside the app.

Your coordinates are never transmitted. There is no endpoint to transmit them
to. You can decline the permission and pick a city from a list instead; the app
works fully either way.

## What the app connects to

Sabeel works offline. The Quran, all 36,104 hadith, the duas, prayer
calculation, the adhan recordings and the fonts are inside the app when you
install it.

Two things reach the network, both only when you ask for them:

- **Recitation audio**, streamed from `everyayah.com` or `mp3quran.net` when you
  press play on a reciter you have not downloaded. Anything you play is kept on
  your device, so it plays offline afterwards.
- **App updates**, through Google Play.

These requests carry nothing but the file being asked for. No identifier of you
or your device is attached by Sabeel. As with any web request, the servers
hosting the audio can see the IP address your connection comes from; that is a
property of the internet rather than something Sabeel adds, and it is the only
reason to think about a VPN if that concerns you.

## Notifications

Prayer notifications are scheduled by your phone's own alarm system, from times
this app calculated on this device. Nothing is pushed from a server — Sabeel has
no push service and no way to send you anything.

## Children

Sabeel is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data at all, it collects
no data about children either.

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Location (coarse and fine) | Prayer times and qibla direction, calculated on the device |
| Notifications | To tell you a prayer time has arrived |
| Exact alarms | So a prayer notification arrives at the minute rather than whenever the system feels like it |
| Run at startup | To restore your prayer schedule after the phone restarts |
| Vibrate | The vibration option for notifications |
| Internet | Streaming recitation, when you press play |
